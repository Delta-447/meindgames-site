Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meind Games LLC ("we", "us") handles information in our mobile games, including Driftline Eternal. By using our games, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, address or contact details through our games. Your game progress, settings and purchases are saved on your device. On iPhone, a copy of your progress is also kept in your device's keychain so it comes back if you reinstall the game, and on Android your progress may be included in your device's Google backup. These copies stay on your device and in your own backups, and are never sent to us.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to answer you.

Advertising

Our games show optional rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads, both operated by Unity Technologies, and their advertising partners. To show and measure ads, these services may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, device type, operating system, IP address and how you interact with ads. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies, including Unity's privacy policy. You can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device's settings (on iPhone under Privacy & Security > Tracking, and on Android under Settings > Google > Ads). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before these services use your data to personalise ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought so it can be added to your game.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices, rights and changes

To erase your game data completely, email us and we will explain how. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you and to object to how it is used. We hold no personal information about you unless you email us; to make a request about a support email, contact us. For data collected by our advertising partners, you can make requests directly to Unity through its privacy policy. We may update this policy from time to time; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other companies.
