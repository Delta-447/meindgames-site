Common questions

I bought tires but didn't receive them.

Purchases are usually added within a few seconds. Make sure you're connected to the internet, then close and reopen the game. If they still haven't arrived, email us with your receipt from the App Store or Google Play and we'll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested through them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, and on Android through your order history in the Google Play Store.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game or change phones?

Usually not. On iPhone, your progress is kept in your phone's keychain, so reinstalling the game brings it back. On Android, it's included in your phone's Google backup, which runs about once a day. If you set up a new phone from a backup of your old one, your progress comes with it. A phone set up from scratch starts the game fresh.

I watched an ad but didn't get my reward.

Rewards are given only when an ad is watched to the end. If an ad closes early or none is available, the reward stays available, so just try again a little later.

How do I change the language, sound or steering sensitivity?

Tap the gear in the bottom-left corner of the main menu to open Settings. You can also replay the tutorial from Settings > General.

The game runs slowly or crashes on my phone.

Close other apps, restart the game and make sure your phone's software is up to date. If the problem continues, email us your device model and what happens, and we'll look into it.
