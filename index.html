Meet Driftline Eternal

Slide to steer. That's the whole control.

A one-thumb drift racer. Slide to throw your car sideways through an endless, ever-changing road, weave past traffic, chain near misses into a combo and see how far you can go.

What's on the road

Everything you'll drive into in endless mode, on purpose or not.

  • Coins

    They come in lines across the road. Spend them on cars and upgrades.

  • Tires

    Rarer than coins. Spend them on extra wheel spins and more.

  • Shield

    Smash straight through traffic. Whatever you hit tumbles off the road.

  • Magnet

    Pulls in every coin nearby, even the ones you were about to miss.

  • Springs

    Flick your thumb up and the car jumps clean over trouble.

  • 2X Coins

    Every coin counts twice for a while.

  • 2X Score

    Every point counts twice for a while.

  • Oil spill

    Spins you out. One quest wants you to hit them anyway.

  • Traffic

    Scrape past for a near-miss bonus. Hit it and you crash.

Plenty to come back for

  • Race mode

    20 races across the countryside, the sunset, the city and the snowy peaks, each against five rivals. Win one to unlock the next.

  • Quests

    Three at a time, from drive 800 m in one run to bank a 600-point drift combo.

  • Daily rewards

    Mystery boxes, coins, token boxes, wheel spins, and on day seven a car.

  • Mystery wheel

    Two free spins every day, with the odds for every prize shown in the game.

  • Upgrades

    Spend coins on your engine, chassis and coin bonus.

  • Weekly ranking

    Pick a username and see how far up the weekly leaderboard you can get.

Free to play, with optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads. Prize odds for every spin and box are shown in the game.
